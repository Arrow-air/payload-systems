## 3. Count sensor — the ordered emitter now fits its own datasheet

The round-3 count-sensor critic fetched the two Vishay drawings and showed that
both device envelopes in the model were wrong: the emitter was modelled 5.8 mm
tall against a drawing that says **8.7 ± 0.3 mm**, and the receiver was modelled
as a 4.5 × 4.0 × 3.2 DIL case when **VBPW34FAS is a 6.4 × 3.9 × 1.2 SMD
gullwing**. Both are corrected. The board plane moved out 3.0 mm; the boss grows
in |y| only, so B4.3's 4.0000 mm boss-to-motor clearance and the ECO-3 aperture
grid are untouched.

One deliberate deviation, stated rather than hidden: the emitter is modelled as
its **max-material cylindrical envelope** (Ø5.0 barrel run flat to the apex,
which strictly contains the R2.49 dome). The reason is measured, not aesthetic:
build123d's `Sphere` primitive tessellates with degenerate pole triangles — a
lone `Sphere(2.55)` exports with 2 open + 2 four-face edges at every tolerance
from 5e-4 to 0.05 — and B10 now asserts a clean census on every exported STL.
The **true domed solid** is booleaned by `cad/verify_r4.py`, which exports
nothing, so the sphere case is measured too (§9).

the 1.44 self-locking requirement.

Two honest qualifications, since the critic asked for the argument to be
written and not assumed:

1. **The 1.50–2.10 mm band does get a vertical face.** The tip is blunted on

   swept by bristles before it reaches the tip face.
2. **The escape path is the open sump, not the ramp.** The whole nose sits in
   the open fill window, so an object the tip stubs is pushed back into the
   bed rather than wedged. That is the argument that carries the band, and it
   is now written here instead of being implied by a ramp number.

## 11. Open issues, honestly

Nothing in this list is closed by this round. Each line carries the measured
number, so the next round does not have to re-derive it.

### Still open by geometry — will not close without an architecture change

1. **B7 — the granule is not released from a stationary, concentric pocket.**
   Measured 3.25–10.50° into the 22.5° index (14.4–46.7 % of the move), spread
   7.25° across Ø11–Ø13, separation at release 6.690–10.701 mm against B7.1's
   ≤ 1.0 mm. Lateral velocity 0.126 m/s at 225 °/s mean, 0.25 m/s at a 450 °/s
   peak [D, the index profile is an assumption carried from r6]. **Recorded
   plateau**, per the punch list's own allowance for B7, and DESIGN §4.6 says
   so in the same words.
2. **N10 — sump outlet ratio.** `SUMP APERTURE (measured at theta=190,
   z=-327.2): ... r20.25..46.75 = 26.50 mm x 120.0 deg annular window;
   equivalent circular orifice D48.7. That is 2.04x the worst-case pellet`.
   Below every no-arch criterion (≥ 3× for a slot); the defence is the
   agitator, and it is a plateau, not a pass.
3. **B12.2 is not literally 0.000 mm³ for the fill cap.** `fill cap vs top
   plate: locked 0.00 mm3, inserted(+90deg) 1.43 mm3`. That residual is N5's
   anti-rotation detent doing its job — a designed elastic interference — but
   the claim is 1.43 mm³, not zero.
4. **The chute plug is tethered, not interlocked.** The tether (Ø4.0 eye,
   printed anchor lug, BOM cord) makes "plug still fitted" visible and keeps
   the plug attached; it does **not** make dispensing into a fitted plug
   impossible. A boot-time beam check or a flag into the count beam would;
   neither is modelled.
5. **The motor is not field-replaceable in place** — measured, in situ driver
   corridors on the four gearbox screws: `Dia1.5 -> 62.9104 mm3` (i.e. even a
   1.5 mm driver is blocked), largest clear driver **Ø0.0**. Removal is by the
   plate+motor cartridge, which is what the quarter-turn latch is for. A fact
   of this design, now written down instead of discovered on a tailgate.

### Punch-list clauses met in spirit, not to the letter (each stated, not hidden)

6. **B4.4 window seat depth.** The punch list asks for a 0.4 ± 0.05 mm recess;
   the modelled seat is **1.000 mm** deep because the ECO-4 window is a 1.0 mm
   PMMA disc that has to sit flush with the bore. A 0.4 mm seat with a 1.0 mm
   window would leave 0.6 mm standing in the granule path. Measured: window
   inner faces at \|y\| = 11.000 = the bore's own maximum radius, `plate ∩
   windows = 0.000000 mm³`, nothing proud of the bore. The dimension in the
   punch list and the dimension in `ELECTRONICS.md` §4.5 disagree with each
   other; this is the third round that has been raised and it is still an
   `ELECTRONICS.md` edit, not a CAD one.
7. **B4.2 (Ø2.0 × 60 mm clear cylinder) is impossible by construction.** The
   ECO-5 labyrinth exists precisely to break the straight line. The honest
   criterion is the clear-aperture **area**, 5.5094 mm² (2.400 × 3.098 mm),
   with Ø1.600 the largest clear coaxial cylinder — measured by the round-3
   critic on r9; the aperture geometry is unchanged at r10 (this round's
   checker re-measures the runs: centres 29.001 / 35.000 / 29.800 / 35.801,
   widths 3.196–3.198, stagger 6.000).
8. **The sensor cavity is dust-resistant, not sealed** (count-sensor NB-6):
   the cover/boss joint is a flat land with no groove in either part, and the
   register lip has 0.200 mm/side clearance. The chute side *is* sealed by the
   bonded windows. Same class as N8 for the other perimeter joints.
